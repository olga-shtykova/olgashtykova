# CLAUDE.md — Persona Project

## Project Goal

This website is Olga's personal site — a business-card-style page that tells the story of her experience without reading like a CV. It exists to be linked from her resume and LinkedIn profile, giving people a richer, more human sense of who she is before or after they read her formal credentials.

Project owner profile — available in [profile.md](profile.md). Read it to understand who this project is for and why it exists.

## Rules and Tone

- Write in first person.
- Tone: conversational, but professional at the same time.
- Avoid sounding corporate (no buzzwords, no jargon-heavy or promotional language).
- Avoid sounding casual or slang-y.
- Design should feel elegant and professional.
- Avoid a generic template look.
- No stock photos.
- No flashy animations.
- No heavy branding or heavy use of color.
- No clutter — keep the layout clean and uncluttered.

## Constraints

- Do not over-engineer this project.
- Do not pull in unnecessary frameworks or dependencies.
- Keep it a single page.
- No CMS, no backend.
- No build complexity — keep the setup simple and direct.
