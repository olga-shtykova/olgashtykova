# Profile

## Who I Am

Hi, I'm **Olga Shtykova**.

I'm a Business Analyst with a software engineering background who enjoys bringing clarity to complex problems. I like working where business and technology meet—understanding what people need, translating those needs into practical solutions, and helping teams move from ideas to successful delivery.

My path into business analysis wasn't a traditional one. I started my career as a .NET Software Engineer before discovering that what I enjoyed most wasn't just building software—it was understanding the problem behind it, asking the right questions, and helping people arrive at the best solution together. My background also includes a degree in Psychology, which shapes how I approach communication and collaboration just as much as my technical education shapes how I understand complex systems. Today, I bring both technical understanding and a people-focused mindset to every project I work on. I'm fluent in English and native in Russian, and I've lived internationally—10 years in the USA, 3 months in Denmark, plus travel across China, India, and much of Europe.

## What I Do

I help teams transform business goals into solutions that are clear, practical, and technically achievable.

Over the past few years, I've worked on enterprise software projects in the ticketing and employee wellness domains, collaborating with stakeholders, product managers, architects, developers, and QA engineers throughout the software delivery lifecycle. Most recently, as a Business Analyst on Ticketmaster UK (via EPAM Systems, Jun 2024 – Jun 2026), I helped deliver an initiative enabling secure third-party API access to Ticketmaster's platform services, coordinating requirements across multiple workstreams and keeping technical and business teams aligned on complex functionality. My work includes discovering and analysing requirements, modelling business processes, refining backlogs, writing user stories and acceptance criteria, supporting delivery, and helping teams maintain a shared understanding of complex functionality.

Before moving into business analysis, I spent over three years (Feb 2021 – Jun 2024) as a Software Engineer on Ticketmaster's event-driven microservices platform, building and maintaining ASP.NET and ASP.NET Core services for a system used by millions of customers—work that also had me maintaining CI/CD pipelines, working with SQL Server, and supporting AWS deployments. Alongside that, I picked up a parallel BA role on EPAM's internal Health and Wellness Portal (Jan 2022 – Apr 2023), and my career began even earlier as a Student Software Engineer on EPAM's training platform, where I built features, fixed defects, and wrote automated tests in the .NET ecosystem.

My engineering background allows me to communicate naturally with development teams while keeping business value at the centre of every conversation. I enjoy acting as the bridge between different perspectives and creating the clarity that helps projects move forward.
